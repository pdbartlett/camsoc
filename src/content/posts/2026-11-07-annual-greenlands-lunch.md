**Our star speaker this year is Edward Lucas Esq., Barrister-at-Law.**

**Subject: "Happenstance – the lives and lives of an Irish barrister in London."**

On leaving school Edward joined the Army and ended up serving in his native Northern Ireland during "the Troubles". He then went to Oxford and emerged with a First in History. This led to a spell teaching in public schools such as Bradfield and Eton before he decided on a career at the Bar, where he is much in demand. He occasionally sits as a Judge.

Your Chairman can testify that Edward has an inexhaustible fund of witty reminiscences, so those attending are in for a very amusing 30-40 minutes, though keeping him down to 40 minutes will be a tough ask.

As usual, lunch starts with a welcome drink (prosecco / fruit juice), followed by three courses from the buffet and finishing with cheese and coffee. White / red wine is also included. We return to Greenlands each year because the quality is excellent. The Society has funds to subsidise the cost, so for members (Cambridge alumni on the mailing list together with spouse) the charge is £49 per person. Feel free to bring a friend or relative, in which case the charge is £56. Dress code for gentlemen: jacket and tie.
