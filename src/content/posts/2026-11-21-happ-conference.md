**HAPP ONE-DAY CONFERENCE: "Light, Mind and Image: A History of Visual Illusion"**

**IN PERSON AND LIVESTREAMED ONLINE on Saturday 21st November 2026 from 10.30 am - 5.00 pm GMT**

From rainbows, mirages and coloured Moons to perspective painting, Op Art and virtual reality, the manipulation of light and image has fascinated general observers, scientists and artists for centuries. This conference traces the history of visual illusion from antiquity through the development of optics, exploring how the physics of light explains natural optical phenomena, image formation and the creation of convincing visual effects. From mirrors, lenses and the camera obscura to photography, computational imaging and augmented reality, the conference will examine how advances in optical science have continually transformed the ways we perceive, represent and experience the visual world.
